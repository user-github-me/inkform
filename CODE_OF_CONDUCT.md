# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical ideas directly,
accept constructive feedback, and make room for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content, and disclosure
of another person's private information or handwriting are not acceptable.
Do not pressure anyone to share personal writing samples to obtain support.
Use synthetic reproductions for public discussions.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces managed by the maintainers. Report a concern privately to a
maintainer; if no private channel is listed, request one without repeating
sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, explain a correction, restrict
participation, or remove participants when needed. They should handle reports
discreetly, consider the context and impact, and apply these expectations
consistently.
